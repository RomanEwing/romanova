Once we verify your cards, you get 60% of market value, paid digitally via Venmo, Zelle, or PayPal. We contact you before anything is finalized.

What We Buy

Minimum Value

We buy cards priced at $10 or more (TCGPlayer market). Cards under $10 are returned or discarded with permission.

Condition

Near Mint (NM) and Lightly Played (LP) preferred. Moderately Played (MP) at reduced rate. HP and Damaged generally declined.

60% Cash

60% of TCGPlayer market price, paid digitally via Venmo, Zelle, or PayPal once we verify your cards. No store credit — just money sent to you.

Packing Instructions

  1. Sleeve every card in a penny sleeve, then place in a top loader or card saver.
  2. Organize by your list so cards are easy to verify on arrival.
  3. Wrap in bubble wrap or pad the package to prevent bending in transit.
  4. Use a rigid mailer or small box — no plain envelopes for valuable cards.
  5. Write the submission ID () on the outside of the package.
  6. Ship with tracking (USPS, UPS, FedEx, etc.). You pay for shipping. Consider insuring the package.
  7. Enter your tracking number below after you drop it off.
